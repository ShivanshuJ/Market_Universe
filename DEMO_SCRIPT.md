# Demo script (2:00) - Market Universe, team Z combinator

Before recording: server running (`PORT=8000 python -m app.server`), browser at 1280x720, dark, zoom 100%.
Run **Groww** once beforehand so every call is cached and the reveal is instant. Hide `.env`, `team.local.json` and any terminal showing the key.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:12 | Landing: "Where does your company stand?" | "Every company pays an analyst to answer three questions: who are my rivals, who is heading where I am heading, and who fell. We replaced that analyst. You only type your company." |
| 0:12-0:25 | Type **Groww**, press Enter. The five-step line glows, then the scene flies in. | "From live Crustdata hiring data it reads Groww's own hiring to work out what Groww is building, then finds the rest by itself." |
| 0:25-0:55 | **Present.** Point at the centre, the rings and the colours. Click **Navi**; the side rail fills; hover it open and show "What this tells you". | "Distance is the size gap, colour is twelve-month growth. Two of eight rivals are growing faster than Groww. Click one and you get the profile: biggest team, employee rating gap, latest round, open roles. Every number is computed in code, not written by a model." |
| 0:55-1:10 | Press the right arrow. **Future.** Click **SMC Private Wealth**; read "Not a rival yet because...". | "Future: companies outside the rivals that already build what Groww builds. Both the team titles and the open roles must match, so a keyword alone is not enough." |
| 1:10-1:25 | Click **Past**. Click **Open Financial Technologies**. | "Past: companies that were big and shrank: 694 people down to 393. The curve is data; the cause only appears as linked reports, never guessed. Acquired companies are flagged, because a drop can be integration, not failure." |
| 1:25-1:45 | In the card or rail click **Deep analysis**. Scroll to the claim court: **502 / 419 joiners -> 217 real hires**. | "And we attack our own numbers. A naive tool says 419 people joined. Our court finds 202 were promotions or restructures, so the real figure is 217. Each claim is killed, weakened or survived, with the tests shown." |
| 1:45-1:55 | Hover the thin **API calls** bar at the bottom. | "Nothing is hidden: every API call is logged, live or cached, with its credits. Repeat runs cost almost nothing, and a failed run resumes where it stopped." |
| 1:55-2:00 | Click the **Team** button; show "Z combinator" and four members. | "Market Universe. Your competitive analyst, replaced. Team Z combinator." |

If asked about a changed fact or a failure: kill the server mid-run, restart, press Resume; finished steps are skipped.
Honest limits to mention if asked: direction is inferred from hiring, not stated strategy; headcount comes from public profiles so recent months can lag; coverage links are unverified.
